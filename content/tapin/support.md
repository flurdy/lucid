## Common Questions

### How do I add a contact?

Tap the "+" button on the home screen to add a new contact with their name, phone number, and your pre-written messages.

### How do I send a message?

Tap on a contact, choose a message, and select whether to send via SMS or WhatsApp. The message will open in your device's native messaging app.

### Is my data stored online?

No. All your contacts and messages are stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers.

### How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data. You can also clear individual contacts and messages from within the App.
