## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TapIn ("the App") handles your information. TapIn is a quick-message app for sending pre-written messages to your favorite contacts via SMS and WhatsApp.

## Data We Collect

TapIn is designed with your privacy in mind. The App:

- **Does not collect personal data** — your contacts, messages, and preferences stay on your device
- **Does not require an account** — no email, name, or login needed
- **Does not track your activity** — no analytics or usage tracking
- **Does not use advertising** — no ad networks or ad identifiers

## Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

- Your saved contacts and message templates
- Your app preferences and settings

This data is not transmitted to any server and is not accessible to us.

## Third-Party Services

TapIn uses the following third-party services:

- **SMS and WhatsApp** — when you send a message, it is sent through your device's native SMS or WhatsApp app. We do not intercept or store these messages.
- **Expo/React Native** — the App is built using Expo and React Native. These frameworks may collect anonymous crash reports and diagnostics. See [Expo's privacy policy](https://expo.dev/privacy) for details.

## Children's Privacy

TapIn does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
