## Common Questions

### How do I add a food item?

Tap the "+" button on the home screen to add a new item with its name, category (fridge, pantry, or freezer), and expiration date.

### How do I see what's expiring soon?

The home screen shows your items sorted by expiration date, with items expiring soonest at the top.

### Is my data stored online?

No. All your food items and preferences are stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers.

### How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data. You can also delete individual items from within the App.
